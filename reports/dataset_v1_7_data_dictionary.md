## 3. Core Entity Master Tables (`entities/`)

- **`products.parquet` (3,643 rows × 30 columns):** Master canonical product entity registry with 100% honest null seller_id (`HONEST_NULL_AGGREGATE_MERCHANT`).
- **`listings.parquet` (3,643 rows × 18 columns):** Marketplace listing entities linking `seller_id` foreign keys to platform aggregate nodes.
- **`brands.parquet` (1,593 rows × 9 columns):** Canonical brand entity registry.
- **`sellers.parquet` (4 rows × 19 columns):** Certified platform aggregate seller entities and unresolved pool.
- **`platforms.parquet` (3 rows × 10 columns):** Platform operator corporate entity registry.
- **`variants.parquet` (3,643 rows × 12 columns):** Product variant hierarchy.

---

## 4. Evidence Observation Tables (`evidence/`)

- **`price_observations.parquet` (3,667 rows × 14 columns):** Append-only price observations with normalized INR values and declared MRPs.
- **`reviews.parquet` (3,659 rows × 15 columns):** Verified customer reviews with star ratings and timestamps.
- **`images.parquet` (4,708 rows × 12 columns):** Scraped physical hero image assets and file hashes.
- **`title_token_packaging_proxy.parquet` (1,000 rows × 8 columns):** Isolated lexical title-token proxy records.

---

## 5. Derived Intelligence Tables (`derived_evidence/`)

- **`review_nlp_features.parquet` (3,659 rows × 56 columns):** Full NLP tokenization, ABSA polarities, spans, and duplicate clusters.
- **`image_intelligence_features.parquet` (4,708 rows × 19 columns):** Perceptual dHash, dimensions, visual clusters, and reuse flags.
- **`image_similarity_pairs.parquet` (2,936 rows × 13 columns):** Cross-product image pairs with centered ResNet-18 cosine similarities.
- **`pricing_features.parquet` (3,643 rows × 25 columns):** Robust z-scores, non-parametric IQR corridors, and discount anomaly flags.
- **`pricing_category_benchmarks.parquet` (343 rows × 10 columns):** Subcategory robust non-parametric corridors.
- **`pricing_brand_benchmarks.parquet` (1,608 rows × 11 columns):** Brand non-parametric benchmarks with reliability flags ($\ge 5$).
- **`seller_features.parquet` (3 rows × 21 columns):** Operational catalog, price spread, and fulfillment profiles for platform seller pools.
- **`multimodal_evidence_records.parquet` (6,064 rows × 13 columns):** Unified atomic multimodal evidence layer.

---

## 6. Heterogeneous Property Graph (`relationships/`)

- **`entity_relationships.parquet` (41,372 rows × 11 columns):** Complete property graph connecting Products, Listings, Brands, Platforms, Sellers, Images, Reviews, and Prices across 12 semantic types with zero nulls and 100% referential integrity.
