## 1. Reconciliation of `product_review_features.parquet` (27,789 rows) vs. Usable Review Evidence (3,627 products)

### Provenance and Temporal Context
The table [`data/processed/product_review_features.parquet`](file:///c:/Users/shruti/Desktop/project/ai-based-marketplace-analyzer/data/processed/product_review_features.parquet) was generated in [`notebooks/04_nlp_analysis.ipynb`](file:///c:/Users/shruti/Desktop/project/ai-based-marketplace-analyzer/notebooks/04_nlp_analysis.ipynb) (Cell 23) prior to the integration of the Indian marketplace datasets (Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra).

At the time of its creation, `canonical_products.parquet` contained **27,789 products** spanning three US marketplaces:
- **Walmart:** 22,074 products
- **Amazon US:** 5,466 products
- **BestBuy:** 249 products
- **Total:** $22,074 + 5,466 + 249 = \mathbf{27,789 \text{ products}}$.

When the 3,500 Indian marketplace products were later added to `canonical_products.parquet` ($1,500 \text{ Amazon India} + 1,000 \text{ Flipkart} + 1,000 \text{ Myntra}$), the canonical catalog expanded to **31,289 products**. The Indian catalog was not back-filled into `product_review_features.parquet` because no customer reviews were collected for those platforms.

### Definition and Verification of `review_count`
In `notebooks/04_nlp_analysis.ipynb` (Cell 23), `review_count` is constructed via:
```python
review_counts = reviews.groupby("canonical_product_id").size().rename("review_count")
product_review_base = products[["canonical_product_id", "marketplace", "category", "title"]].merge(
    review_counts, on="canonical_product_id", how="left"
)
product_review_base["review_count"] = product_review_base["review_count"].fillna(0).astype("int64")
```

Thus, `review_count` strictly represents the count of associated textual customer reviews in [`data/processed/canonical_reviews.parquet`](file:///c:/Users/shruti/Desktop/project/ai-based-marketplace-analyzer/data/processed/canonical_reviews.parquet). Products without reviews receive `review_count = 0`.

### Exact Breakdown of the 27,789 Rows:
- **Products with Usable Reviews (`review_count > 0`): Exactly 3,627 products**
  - **BestBuy:** 249 products (all 249 have reviews; 44,965 reviews total; avg 180.6 reviews/product; `DENSE_MULTI_REVIEW`).
  - **Amazon US:** 3,378 products (10,651 reviews total; avg 3.15 reviews/product; 331 `DENSE_MULTI_REVIEW` + 3,047 `SPARSE_SINGLE_REVIEW`).
  - **Walmart:** 0 products have reviews.
  - $\sum \text{review\_count} = 44,965 + 10,651 = \mathbf{55,616 \text{ reviews}}$ (100% exact match to `canonical_reviews.parquet`).
- **Products with Zero Reviews (`listing_state == 'ZERO_REVIEWS'`): Exactly 24,162 products**
  - **Walmart:** 22,074 products
  - **Amazon US:** 2,088 products
- **Products Missing / Uncovered (`NOT_COVERED`): 3,500 products**
  - **Amazon India:** 1,500 products
  - **Flipkart:** 1,000 products
  - **Myntra:** 1,000 products

**Conclusion:** The **3,627 product count is 100% correct and verified**. The 27,789 rows in `product_review_features.parquet` preserve zero-review listings for the 24,162 US products evaluated, while the remaining 3,500 products were never covered by review scrapers.

## 2. Provenance and Role of `data/interim/product_pricing_cohort.parquet` (17,704 products)

### Origin and Construction
The file [`data/interim/product_pricing_cohort.parquet`](file:///c:/Users/shruti/Desktop/project/ai-based-marketplace-analyzer/data/interim/product_pricing_cohort.parquet) was generated by [`src/build_pricing_cohort.py`](file:///c:/Users/shruti/Desktop/project/ai-based-marketplace-analyzer/src/build_pricing_cohort.py) directly from the raw Amazon All Beauty metadata file [`data/raw/amazon/all_beauty_metadata.parquet`](file:///c:/Users/shruti/Desktop/project/ai-based-marketplace-analyzer/data/raw/amazon/all_beauty_metadata.parquet).

In that raw vendor file, 17,704 unique `parent_asin` records possessed valid, positive numeric prices. The script computed category-level median prices, interquartile ranges (IQR), Tukey fences, robust Z-scores via Median Absolute Deviation (MAD), and outlier classification flags (`PRICE_DUMP_ANOMALY` vs. `PRICE_GOUGE_ANOMALY`).

### Canonical Catalog Overlap
Cross-referencing the 17,704 `parent_asin`s in `product_pricing_cohort.parquet` against `canonical_products.parquet` reveals:
- **Canonical Overlap:** Exactly **1,800 products** (`source_product_id`).
- **Non-Canonical Products:** **15,904 products** (89.83% of the file) were **never ingested** into the canonical catalog.
- The 1,800 canonical products match the Amazon US Beauty subset with valid prices in `canonical_products.parquet`.

### Pipeline Role: Legacy Interim Experiment
A search across all active modules in `src/` (`src/risk/engine.py`, `src/features/pricing.py`, `src/nlp/`, `src/vision/`) confirms that:
- `product_pricing_cohort.parquet` is **NOT consumed by any production pipeline**.
- It is only referenced in data validation scripts (`src/validate_dataset_integrity.py`, `src/generate_dataset_manifest.py`).
- **Verdict:** `product_pricing_cohort.parquet` is an **interim exploratory artifact** from early Amazon All Beauty price anomaly experiments. It is **not** an authoritative catalog table and must not be used as the curated cohort source.

## 3. Sampling Strategy for the 500 Amazon US Review Products (Sub-tier C4)

### Candidate Population Pool
Across Amazon US, 3,378 products have `review_count > 0`.
Excluding products already assigned to higher tiers:
- **Tier A (Gold Triple Modality):** 300 products
- **Tier B3 (Dual NLP + Price):** 1 product
- **Eligible Candidates for Sub-tier C4:** Exactly **3,077 products**

### Review-Count Distribution of Eligible Candidates
Inspection of these 3,077 candidates reveals that they are **not strictly restricted to single-review products**:
- **Single-Review Products (`SPARSE_SINGLE_REVIEW`, `review_count == 1`):** **3,034 products** (98.60%)
- **Multi-Review Products (`DENSE_MULTI_REVIEW`, `review_count > 1`):** **43 products** (1.40%)
  - 39 products with 2 reviews
  - 3 products with 3 reviews
  - 1 product with 4 reviews

### Candidate Category Diversity
Unlike the Gold cohort (which is 100% Beauty), these 3,077 review-bearing products span five diverse categories:
- **Home & Kitchen:** 682 products (12 multi-review)
- **Toys & Games:** 652 products (10 multi-review)
- **Books & Stationery:** 603 products (5 multi-review)
- **Electronics:** 579 products (12 multi-review)
- **Clothing & Fashion:** 561 products (4 multi-review)

### Proposed Representative Sampling Strategy (Target: Exactly 500 Products)
To maximize signal richness and avoid category bias:
1. **Preserve 100% of Multi-Review Products (43 products):** Include all 43 products with $\ge 2$ reviews to preserve intra-listing review similarity signals (`max_intra_listing_similarity`) and sentiment discordance outside the Beauty category.
2. **Category-Stratified Sampling for Single-Review Products (457 products):** Draw $457 / 5 \approx 91\text{--}92$ products proportionally across the 5 categories:
   - **Home & Kitchen:** 12 multi-review + 91 single-review = **103 products**
   - **Toys & Games:** 10 multi-review + 91 single-review = **101 products**
   - **Electronics:** 12 multi-review + 91 single-review = **103 products**
   - **Books & Stationery:** 5 multi-review + 92 single-review = **97 products**
   - **Clothing & Fashion:** 4 multi-review + 92 single-review = **96 products**
   - **Subtotal:** $43 + 457 = \mathbf{500 \text{ products}}$.
3. **Reproducibility:** Perform sampling using a fixed deterministic random seed (`seed = 42`) on sorted `canonical_product_id`s.

## 4. Assessment of the 300 Gold Products (Tier A)

### Suitability for End-to-End Development
All 300 products in Tier A were validated against multi-modality criteria:
- **NLP Density:** Every product is classified as `DENSE_MULTI_REVIEW` with between **9 and 40 customer reviews** (mean = 25.1, median = 22.0). All have verified ratings, timestamps, sentiment scores, and intra-listing similarity features.
- **Computer Vision Evidence:** Every product has verified 512-dimensional unit L2-normalized pooled ResNet-18 embeddings derived from **1 to 21 usable images** (mean = 7.55, median = 6.0). 254 out of 300 products have OCR-readable packaging text.
- **Price Intelligence:** Every product has a valid numeric price in USD (min = \$3.75, median = \$16.83, max = \$226.00).
- **Brand Richness:** Represents **260 distinct brands** across 300 listings (e.g. Clinique, Tarte, Duke Cannon).

### Category and Marketplace Limitation
- **Marketplace:** 100% Amazon US.
- **Category:** 100% `Beauty & Personal Care` (subcategory: `All Beauty`).
- **Architectural Implications:**
  - **Suitable for:** Benchmarking full 5-modality fusion, testing discordance between visual packaging and review sentiment, evaluating multi-review manipulation algorithms, and running ablation studies.
  - **Not suitable for:** Cross-marketplace matching, cross-currency exchange calibrations (USD vs. INR), or generalizing price anomaly models across disparate product taxonomies (e.g. consumer electronics vs. apparel). Those dynamics are explicitly tested by Tiers B and C.
